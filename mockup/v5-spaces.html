Translation of Zen's sidebar into the existing 47% search sheet: Essentials (global, capped 12) → current-space indicator (icon + name + counts; its chevron collapses pinned tabs, exactly like Zen's workspace indicator) → Pins · space (folders: 24dp 2×2 favicon preview, children indented 18dp with a 1dp rule) → Open tabs for this space → Not synced placeholder → bottom workspace icon strip (one 32dp disc per space; active uses the space gradient, inactive are greyscaled at 0.65, + creates a new space, hold to edit/reorder/delete). Space colours (--c1/--c2) tint the indicator and the selected tab row, over the existing glass blur/tint/grain. The private-mask badge stays pinned to the field's right edge. Reduced motion skips the 160ms chevron/name transitions. Not included (Phase 1 non-goals): split view, glance, container isolation, accounts, network sync.
