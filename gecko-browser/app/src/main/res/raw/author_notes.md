# Author Notes

## ✨ Why this exists

huriZen is a love letter to the web browser — a fast, private, GeckoView-powered browser that gives you the speed of a modern rendering engine without giving away your data.

## 🧑‍💻 What I care about

- Privacy first: no telemetry, no trackers, no ads
- Simplicity: one pill, one focus
- Extensions done right: AMO-compatible, signed, yours to manage

## 🛠️ Notes for users

- horiZen is in beta. Expect rough edges. Reporting them helps.
- Add-ons from addons.mozilla.org are supported.
- Language models for translation are downloaded on-device; nothing is sent to the cloud.

## 📌 Roadmap

- Stable channel after incubation
- More engines and suggestion sources
- Immersive reading mode
